# Syncthing GUI overrides

`default/index.html` and the translation files in `default/assets/lang/` are
derived from Syncthing v2.1.7, commit `d6d0181f7d466381c4b39ec8fd944c364f939b32`.
They remain subject to the Mozilla Public License 2.0; its text is in `LICENSE`.
The rest of the interface and its third-party dependencies are served by the
unmodified Syncthing binary and retain their respective licenses.

CoSyncX changes the navigation and branding and adds a notice about the
unverified Codex chat integration. `default/assets/css/cosyncx.css` is a
CoSyncX addition under the project's MIT license.
